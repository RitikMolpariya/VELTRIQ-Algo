01 / PROFITABILITY

Positive net result, modest profit factor

The combined workbook reports net profit of 64,783.64 account-currency units and a 1.23 profit factor. Gross profit and gross loss are both substantial relative to net profit, so execution costs and changes in trade distribution could materially affect realized results.

02 / RISK-ADJUSTED

Metrics are useful, not guarantees

Reported daily annualized Sharpe and Sortino ratios are 1.40 and 3.01. These depend on the source methodology and sample period. Maximum drawdown is based on closed trades and may not capture intratrade equity drawdown.

03 / DIRECTION & VARIATION

Long and short both contribute

Long trades report 30,995.02 net P&L across 4,425 trades; short trades report 33,788.62 across 4,363 trades. Annual and monthly results vary, including negative months. Risk limits and ongoing validation remain important.

Performance data note

GOOD TO KNOW / 05

Frequently asked
questions.

A few useful details before you explore the toolkit.

Are these products financial advice? +

No. Website content and tools are for informational and technical purposes. They are not individualized investment advice, a recommendation to trade, or a promise of results.

Do you offer payments or subscriptions here? +

Not in this version. This website is a product and contact showcase. You can add a secure checkout later if your business model and compliance requirements support it.

Can tools connect TradingView and MetaTrader 5? +

Some workflows can route alerts between platforms, but compatibility depends on the script, execution bridge, broker, account settings, and testing. Never assume an alert guarantees an order was placed.

Are profits guaranteed? +

No. Trading involves substantial risk, including the possible loss of capital. Historical tests and simulated results do not guarantee future performance.
